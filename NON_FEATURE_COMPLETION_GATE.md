# The Styliste — Non-Feature Completion Gate

Status: **creator-approved production authority for GDD v8**  
Creator direction confirmed: July 25, 2026  
Decision-status rule: **creator-approved decisions are binding; labeled implementation recommendations remain provisional until tested or separately approved.**

A build is not complete because routes, schemas, providers, screens, content, and tests exist. The following product qualities require observed evidence.

| Area | Completion evidence |
|---|---|
| Core fantasy | Unprompted young-adult testers say they built or are building their own luxury fashion empire |
| Tone | Players identify aspiration and glamour, with mature drama and sharp critique, without perceiving casino pressure, childishness, or bleakness |
| World | The game feels realistic with heightened fashion-industry drama |
| Visual authorship | Major screens and assets are coherent, non-generic, and pass human art-direction review |
| City dialect | Kingston is culturally credible; later city art styles differ intentionally while remaining recognizably The Styliste |
| Fashion taste | At least one garment or collection is genuinely desirable, construction-aware, and not dependent on rarity framing |
| Character identity | Luxe, Vex, and Seraphine are recognizable by silhouette, writing, behavior, and sound |
| Luxe integrity | Luxe is useful, honest, non-pushy, and never perceived as a shop mascot or notification bot |
| Vex integrity | Vex is sharp, fair, evidence-based, and never abusive or identity-directed |
| Rival quality | Seraphine creates strategic counterplay and dynamic relationship memory rather than scripted humiliation |
| Emotional arc | Work, anticipation, spectacle, reflection, failure, recovery, rivalry, and aspiration are present |
| Game feel | Priority interactions have authored touch, motion, sound, haptic, and fallbacks |
| World life | Decisions visibly alter people, spaces, stock, staff, stores, relationships, or environment |
| Writing | Dialogue is specific, fashion-literate, character-distinct, non-preachy, and grounded in legitimate evidence |
| Audio | Kingston, Aurelian ceremony, core states, and major characters are audibly distinct without copying references |
| Ownership | Players can explain what their House believes, who it serves, and what makes it recognizable |
| Architect presentation | Business play is visually active and never degrades into spreadsheet-only interaction |
| Trust | Prices, outcomes, data, reports, recovery, and support are understandable and non-manipulative |
| Solo viability | Core progression remains complete without compulsory multiplayer or social labor |
| Reliability | Save, resume, reconnect, offline settlement, entitlement, and error recovery are dependable |
| Accessibility | Core loop works with text scaling, reduced motion, screen-reader semantics, and audio alternatives |
| Performance | Profile/release evidence meets the reference-device contract |
| Marketability | A truthful 30-second gameplay capture communicates the luxury fashion empire fantasy |
| Retention | Testers voluntarily begin another Collection Cycle |
| Sustainability | The solo developer can maintain the enabled content and operations |
| Cultural review | Kingston and later city references pass informed human review |
| Security | Required RLS, authority, abuse, backup, and incident gates pass |

## Automatic blockers

The release is blocked when the current build presents any of the following as final quality:

- generic AI art or disconnected asset-dump presentation;
- cheap sexualization, body shaming, or childish dress-up aesthetics;
- casino-like monetization, P2W, fake scarcity, or paid crisis rescue;
- shallow melodrama, random soap-opera conflict, or relentlessly bleak tone;
- stereotyped Kingston or another city;
- Vex abuse or Luxe fake flattery;
- spreadsheet-only Architect gameplay;
- forced multiplayer, forced social spam, or mandatory Maison labor;
- hidden authoritative outcomes or client-owned competitive/economic state;
- unverified claims of device, production, legal, security, or human-validation readiness.

## Evidence labels

Use only the evidence labels authorized by `VERIFICATION_PROTOCOL.md`. Static inspection cannot be promoted to runtime, device, production, penetration-test, cultural-review, or human-validation evidence.

## Smaller-release rule

Any failed category may block promotion when it affects the core fantasy, player safety, fairness, security, accessibility, truthful marketing, cultural integrity, or maintainability. A smaller polished release is preferred to waiving the gate.
