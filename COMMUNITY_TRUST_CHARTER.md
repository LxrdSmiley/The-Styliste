# The Styliste — Community and Trust Charter

Status: **creator-approved production authority for GDD v8**  
Creator direction confirmed: July 25, 2026  
Decision-status rule: **creator-approved decisions are binding; labeled implementation recommendations remain provisional until tested or separately approved.**  
Primary audience: young adults

## Purpose

The Styliste combines aspiration, glamour, taste, culture, business, and competition. Community systems must support identity and critique without normalizing cruelty, plagiarism, exploitation, coercive social labor, body judgment, or pay-to-win status.

The game remains fully viable for solo players. Social participation may enrich the world but cannot become mandatory labor, forced recruitment, spam, or a prerequisite for core progression.

## Community values

- Distinct House identity.
- Constructive, evidence-based criticism.
- Fair competition and seasonal mobility.
- Attribution, consent, and creative ownership.
- Cultural curiosity with responsibility.
- Knowledge sharing and mentorship.
- Collaboration without forced labor.
- Recovery after mistakes.
- Respect for privacy, identity, body, and boundaries.
- Solo-player dignity and complete viability.

## Conduct requirements

Prohibit harassment, hate, sexual exploitation, threats, stalking, doxxing, impersonation, scams, plagiarism, non-consensual sharing, targeted humiliation, body shaming, vote manipulation, coercive recruitment, forced daily labor, and exploitation of minors or vulnerable players.

## Fashion criticism boundary

Critique may address garment design, silhouette, construction, material, price, campaign, cultural context, originality, execution, and House claims.

Critique may not target a real person’s body, protected traits, disability, identity, accent, income, class, ethnicity, sexuality, gender expression, or presumed human worth.

Vex’s sharp professional boundary also governs player-facing critique tools and moderation policy.

## Attribution and plagiarism

- Provide clear reporting categories and evidence preservation.
- Distinguish inspiration, shared tradition, common motif, licensed use, collaboration, transformative work, and direct copying.
- Offer appeals and contextual human review.
- Avoid public accusation mechanics that encourage dogpiling.
- Record collaboration credits and agreed ownership.

## Cultural concerns

Reports involving appropriation, false affiliation, sacred or restricted symbols, unpaid labor, exploitative collaboration, or community misrepresentation require contextual human review. Automated moderation may triage but cannot make final cultural judgments alone.

Kingston and every future city require informed local review. No city is treated as an aesthetic resource, stereotype, inferior market, or disposable stepping stone.

## Young-adult audience safety

Before open communication features launch, implement age-appropriate defaults, restricted contact discovery, blocking, reporting, rate limits, link controls, privacy settings, trained escalation, retention limits, and legal/guardian review where required by jurisdiction or platform.

Young-adult targeting does not authorize sexualized monetization, predatory parasocial design, manipulative romance spending, or exploitative body presentation.

## Monetization trust

- Clear prices, contents, entitlements, and restoration.
- Clear odds where randomization is legally and ethically permitted.
- No fake discounts, disguised advertisements, or surprise currency conversion.
- No paid crisis solution, score purchase, market-share ceiling, Gala advantage, or immunity from economic risk.
- No humiliation, exclusion, or social pressure used to sell products.
- Honest compensation for verified server-caused loss.
- Payer and nonpayer players retain the same competitive ceiling.

## Anti-compulsion and social-labor rules

- No mandatory daily check-ins for Maison members.
- No punishment for missing a social event or message.
- No forced external social sharing.
- No spam invitation objectives.
- No core system that collapses without a large live population.
- No player may be pressured to perform unpaid moderation or organizational labor to retain rewards.

## Moderation operations

Every enabled social feature requires named human ownership, response targets, escalation, enforcement consistency, appeals, auditability, privacy controls, abuse metrics, and a disable switch. Defer the feature when operational capacity is insufficient.

## Staff conduct

Staff and moderators follow least privilege, confidentiality, conflict-of-interest disclosure, anti-retaliation, evidence standards, audited access, and break-glass review.

## Public communication

Maintenance, incidents, enforcement changes, compensation, and policy updates must be clear, timely, and honest. Do not blame players for server, synchronization, entitlement, or migration failures.
